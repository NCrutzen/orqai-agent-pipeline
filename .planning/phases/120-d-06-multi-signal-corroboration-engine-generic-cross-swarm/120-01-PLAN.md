<objective>
Give the generic Stage-2 resolution engine a config-expressible corroboration capability — the CAPABILITY that must land BEFORE any config seeds it (Pitfall 2: a `corroboration` field on a swarm config that the engine does not read is silently ignored).

When `config.corroboration` is present, `resolveEntity()` runs ALL precedence steps, collects every candidate, then scores deterministically via a new pure helper: boost to `high` on ≥N independent-kind agreement (D-04), demote below AUTO on independent-kind disagreement (D-06), cap a lone sender/domain signal below AUTO while keeping lone strong signals high (D-07). When `config.corroboration` is ABSENT the loop is byte-identical to today (protects no-engine-diff for non-opted-in swarms — CORR-04).

Purpose: deterministic, LLM-free multi-signal corroboration that makes a confident WRONG customer (the cardinal Stage-2 failure) both rarer and falsifiable.
Output: `corroborate.ts` (new pure helper), a `CorroborationConfig` field on `ResolutionConfig`/`EngineConfig`, the engine collect branch, and 4 new unit tests.
</objective>
