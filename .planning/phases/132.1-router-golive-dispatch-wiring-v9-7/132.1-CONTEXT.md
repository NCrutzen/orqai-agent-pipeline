## Phase Boundary

Take the BUILT-but-held info-routing router (Phase 131) + forward side-effect (Phase 132) **live-measurable**. Three pieces:

3. **Shadow-precision gate** — measure router verdict vs operator-actual destination; gate the real Outlook forward on ≥85% precision per mailbox.

**Out of scope:** Phase 133 (NL routing-config + verkoop→order); Phase 134 (ambient-loop). The forward side-effect stays dry-run until the ≥85% gate clears per mailbox.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Ground-truth for shadow precision
- **D-01:** Ground-truth = the operator's correction of the `AI:` routing-category on the email, captured via the **Phase 112.5 Graph delta-sync**. Reuses the Phase 117 swarm-agnostic stamp-back + the 112.5 delta tracker (no new mechanism, no form). **Requires `behavior_tracking_config.enabled=true` on the info@ mailboxes** (currently `false`). The `routing_destinations.outlook_category` field is the labelled measurement axis (per 131 design).

### Go-live gate & sequencing
- **D-02:** **Per-mailbox AUTOMATIC flip** of the Stage-4 forward side-effect: once a mailbox's shadow precision reaches ≥85%, that mailbox's forward goes live automatically (no operator button). smeba and fire-control flip independently. The ≥85% gate IS the safety mechanism. **Plan must add:** a minimum-N guard (don't auto-flip on a tiny sample) + a monitoring/alert + a one-flag revert (dry-run flip-back) since this auto-acts on real forwards.

### Routing-label colour
- **D-03:** **One** distinct, non-grey Outlook preset colour for **all** routing categories (operators distinguish routing labels from the grey `AI:` intent labels at a glance). `AI:` intent categories stay grey. Provision the routing master-category with a `presetColor` via Graph `masterCategories` (colour is a master-category property, not set at message-stamp time). NOT per-bucket, NOT per-brand.

### Dispatch-scope
- **D-04:** Route Stage-1-cleared actionable mail that has a clear destination. **`algemeen`/contactformulier → no action** (stays in info@; not forwarded, not measured). Do not invest in an `algemeen` verdict path here.

### Claude's Discretion
- The exact mechanism for threading `email_id` through the info-routing Stage-3 dispatch (researcher locates where it goes null today; planner picks the structural fix).
- The shadow-precision measurement window + per-mailbox minimum-N before the ≥85% auto-flip can trigger (research-informed; reuse the ROUTE-08 harness from 131).

### Folded Todos
- **Routing labels need a distinct Outlook category colour** (`.planning/todos/pending/2026-06-23-routing-labels-distinct-outlook-category-color.md`) — folded as **D-03**. The todo's solution-hint (provision via `masterCategories.presetColor`) carries forward.
</decisions>
